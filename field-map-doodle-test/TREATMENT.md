# Join the hunt — treatment

Style: Field Map Doodle (own style, this film is its demo). 1080×1920, 30 fps, 118 BPM (beat 0.508 s, bar 2.034 s), 15 bars + tail ≈ 31 s. Music and sound effects only: no voice, no subtitles.

## Structure

One structure (a style demo needs only one): **a zoom from the map**. We start as dots on the field map, iris into one player's phone, tap through Kalikkam's real first launch (splash → three tour cards → "Join the hunt" → Home), pull back out to the map, where a zombie is already closing in. The ending closes the iris into the wordmark's crosshair: map → phone → map → logo.

- **Logline**: a survivor opens Kalikkam for the first time; four taps later she's on the field, and a zombie's crosshair is already on her.
- **Setup** (bars 0–2): the field map, players as dots; a crosshair locks on one of them and opens into her phone.
- **Development** (bars 2–9): the real entry flow, one tap per card, each answered by the characters on screen.
- **Turn** (bars 9–11): pull back to the map; the music drops to a heartbeat; a yellow dot closes in; LOCK-ON.
- **Ending** (bars 11–15): she climbs out of the phone and runs, the zombie climbs after her; the iris closes into the wordmark's O. "Coming to Kochi."

## Benchmark

- **Learn from**: phone-app launch spots that cut on taps (one action per beat, the UI really changing state), and sticker-style character animation that holds and pops rather than floats.
- **Don't take**: any character, logo, UI, music or shot from them, nor the Game Show Flat demo's levels, cards or shouts.
